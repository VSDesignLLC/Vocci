# Vocci Product Briefing \- 260825

# A\. Product \& Value Proposition

## Product Overview

Vocci Ring is an AI Ring that captures real\-world conversations, ideas, and decisions, then makes them usable context when it is time to work with AI\.

It helps users avoid manually reconstructing, exporting, and re\-explaining what happened in real life before AI can be useful\.

## Priority Value Propositions

### Real\-world context for AI

Vocci helps users bring conversations, meetings, ideas, and decisions from real life into their AI workflow\.

AI can already write, summarize, and help move work forward\. Vocci adds the context of what actually happened before the user sits down at a screen\.

### Capture without interrupting the moment

Users can double\-click the ring to start capturing when a conversation or thought is worth keeping\.

The product is designed to let users remain present, rather than reaching for a phone, opening an app, or taking notes in the moment\.

### An AI Ring for everyday use

Vocci combines capture and AI capabilities with an all\-day wearable form factor\.

It is designed for meetings, conversations, spontaneous ideas, and everyday moments that happen away from a desk\.

## Supporting Features

- Double\-click to start recording, with haptic and LED feedback\.

- Offline recording with later phone and cloud sync\.

- Transcription, summaries, action items, speaker labels, export and sharing\.

- Up to 8 hours of continuous recording\.

- IP67 water and dust resistance\.

- Up to 5m effective recording range\.

- 100\+ language support\.

- Titanium inner and outer surfaces\.

- 3–5g weight depending on size\.

- On\-device encryption, permission\-based access, and user\-controlled sync\.

## Product Communication Boundaries

- Do not position Vocci as a voice recorder ring or generic AI hardware\.

- Do not present it only as a meeting\-notes product\.

- Do not use MCP as the primary consumer\-facing benefit\.

- Do not imply automatic syncing, autonomous task completion, or access to conversations without user authorization\.
