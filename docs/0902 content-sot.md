## 5. 新内容 SOT（按目标结构重组）

> 以下为 canonical 内容。英文为现有/拟定文案，可直接用于视觉细化；中文为板块说明。`【待核实】` 标记处需真实素材确认后落定。

### 板块 1 · 首屏 — 主视觉
- **主标题**：Wear the Context.
- **副标题**：The AI Ring that brings real life into ChatGPT & Claude. Your conversations work automatically.
- **CTA**：`[Buy Vocci Ring]` `[See how it works]`

### 板块 2 · Highlights — 使用场景 / 建立产品整体形象

**新增 3 点（置于最前）**

1. **Real-world context for AI**
   Vocci helps users bring conversations, meetings, ideas, and decisions from real life into their AI workflow. AI can already write, summarize, and help move work forward. Vocci adds the context of what actually happened before the user sits down at a screen.
2. **Capture without interrupting the moment**
   Users can double-click the ring to start capturing when a conversation or thought is worth keeping. The product is designed to let users remain present, rather than reaching for a phone, opening an app, or taking notes in the moment.
3. **An AI Ring for everyday use**
   Vocci combines capture and AI capabilities with an all-day wearable form factor. It is designed for meetings, conversations, spontaneous ideas, and everyday moments that happen away from a desk.

**原有点（置于其后）**

4. **产品形象**：Jewelry first, hardware second. Mirror-polished metal, shaped like a pebble you want to keep turning over. The most advanced thing you carry shouldn't look like it. 　
5. **使用场景**：In the room, not in your notes. The conversation ends, nothing gets missed. Vocci captures every decision while you stay fully present — eyes up, hands free, mind on the person in front of you.
   - 支撑句：Speaker-labeled, in 100+ languages. Summaries, action items and minutes — review what was said instead of replaying recordings.

### 板块 3 · 细节解决方法 — 4 个使用步骤
- 区块标题：A gesture away. The rest follows. ／ Two gestures on the ring. Everything downstream is automatic.
- **01 — Double-tap · Start recording.** Vocci listens and turns everything around you into context — privately and securely.
- **02 — Transcribed · Everything, written down.** Summaries, action items and speaker-labeled minutes. Automatically. In 100+ languages.
- **03 — Long-press · Send it anywhere.** Notes, contacts or summaries — to Claude, Notion, Codex, or whoever needs it.
- **04 — Agent-ready · Your assistant takes it from here.** Plan your day, draft emails, build on your ideas — hands-free.

### 板块 4 · 产品细节 — 工艺呈现
- 区块标题：Designed for all-day wear. Even showers. ／ Aerospace-grade titanium, hypoallergenic and water-resistant. Put it on Monday and forget it's there.
- **戒指规格**：Material 钛(内外) · Weight 3–5 g · Width/Thickness 6.8 mm / 2.85 mm · Sizes US 6–15 · Skin Hypoallergenic · Water IP67
- **充电盒**：Magnetic charging · USB-C · 3 full charges · 0–80% in 30 min · 62 mm
- **工艺/表面**：Mirror-polished, jewelry-grade finish（来自 §3）
- **三色**：silver / black / gold
- **能力指标**（自 §7 迁入）：8 hrs 连续录音 · IP67 防水 · 5 m 拾音距离 · 100+ 语言

- 区块标题：**Why buy from Vocci**
