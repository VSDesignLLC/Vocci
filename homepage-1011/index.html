Overview

From conversation to AI workflow.

Vocci is an AI note-taking ring that captures conversations and ideas, turns recordings into transcripts and summaries, and gives your AI tools the context to help with what comes next.

01

Start recording in a moment.

Double-click to start or stop recording—whether it’s a meeting, a conversation, or an idea worth keeping.

02

Get AI help on demand.

Press and hold the ring to ask Vocci AI a question or hand off a task, without reaching for your phone.

03

Turn notes into next steps.

Have Vocci AI draft an email or plan from what was said—or connect the AI tools you already use to those same notes.
